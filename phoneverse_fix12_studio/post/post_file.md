Description: A two-pound text can empty your bank account. It lands in Black Friday week, says a parcel missed you and asks for a redelivery fee, then sends you to a copycat page that wants your card details. Evri says it never charges UK customers for redelivery, so a fee request by text is a scam. With your card details, scammers can ring you posing as your bank and push you to move your savings to a safe account. Barclays research published on 13 November found 34 percent of UK adults have been targeted by a shopping scam, lost money to one or know someone caught out, ahead of Black Friday on 27 November. On an iPhone with iOS 26 or newer, open Messages, tap Filters, tap Manage Filtering, then turn on Screen Unknown Senders. Texts from numbers you have never messaged go to a separate list and usually don't notify you, though time-sensitive ones like verification codes still can. It is off by default; on iOS 18 it is called Filter Unknown Senders. Real parcel updates from new numbers may land there, so track orders in the shop's own app. Forward scam texts to 7726 for free, and report losses to Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040. Already paid?
