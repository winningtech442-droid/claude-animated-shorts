# Phoneverse: "Your Phone Has Been Found" (draft 1)

**Premise:** You start explaining where snatched phones go, your own phone gets snatched mid-sentence, and three months later a text says it's been found.

**Real safety point:** after a theft, scammers send fake "your lost phone has been found" texts. The link is a fake sign-in page. If you enter your Apple Account password, they can remove Activation Lock and sell the phone.

**Format:** fake explainer (0:00-0:14) → interrupt (her phone gets snatched mid-line) → the camera follows the phone instead of her → time jump → the trap → Jess's twist → cliffhanger. Target **63-66 s**, vertical, Rocketbox cast, London.

## Why this topic (data, 26 Nov 2026)
- **Our best video by far** is the phone-theft one (5 Nov, shoulder surfing + Stolen Device Protection): **16,012 views, 222 shares, 22 s average watch**. No other post in 30 days passed 900 views.
- Our scam-only videos (Amazon call, WhatsApp, digital wallet, £2 text) all sat at **95-214 views**. This topic starts as a **theft** story, which works for us, and ends in a **scam**, so the theft side pulls viewers in.
- **Not logged yet:** we haven't covered what happens *after* a theft.
- **Real and recent:**
  - The Met's Operation Echosteep: a gang suspected of smuggling up to 40,000 stolen phones from the UK to China. It started from a box of stolen iPhones bound for Hong Kong, found near Heathrow.
  - The Swiss NCSC (Nov 2025), Malwarebytes and TechRadar have all warned about fake "your iPhone has been found" texts sent to theft victims.
