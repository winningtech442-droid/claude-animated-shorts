# Phoneverse: "Cheers, Biscuit" Part 2, the finale (draft 1)

**Premise:** Jess gave them your password, and now they're coming for your NEW phone.

**Real safety point:** with only your Apple Account password, someone can sign in to Find Devices on iCloud.com **without a verification code** and erase your phones. The fix, in order:
1. Change your password and tap "Remove Other Devices".
2. Sign out of all browsers.

**Decisions (from the questionnaire):**
| | Choice |
|---|---|
| Premise | They have the password. The hooded figure goes for her new phone |
| Cast | You, Jess, the hooded figure (three only) |
| Place | Jess's flat (main set) and the warehouse den, joined by camera dives through the phone/laptop screens |
| Hottest angle | "No code needed": Find Devices skips the six-digit code. The usual found-phone warnings don't cover it |
| Counter | **CLICKS TO ERASE 3 → 2 → 1**, freezes on 1 and cracks |
| Ending | She wins and steals his line, "Cheers, Biscuit." A mild threat text arrives, she screenshots it for the police, and Jess reveals "Biscuit124" |
| Series | Cold open on Part 1's "JESS!" echo. "Oi, camera!" is now an order. You and Jess become the recurring duo |

**Length:** 190 spoken words. Part 1 was voiced at 3.24 words a second while talking, so that's about 57 s of speech. With about 11 s of action beats (dives, the clicking race, the slow turn), it comes to **about 68 s**. That's over a minute, so it qualifies for Creator Rewards.
