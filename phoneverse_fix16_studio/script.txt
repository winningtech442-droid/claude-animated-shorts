Every time you upgrade your iPhone, you throw away real gold.
Real gold. Inside your old phone.
Recycling plants do this. Please don't try it at home.
It only takes three simple steps.
First, the battery comes out. Very, very important.
If it gets crushed or punctured, it can catch fire.
Look closer. These shiny pins? Gold.
Some chips have gold wires thinner than a hair.
Next, the board goes in a liquid. Slowly, slowly, it turns blue.
It falls apart into black, ugly mud.
Once the bubbling stops, the mud starts to sparkle.
Wash it, rinse it, wash it again. A stronger acid turns it yellow.
One more powder. Once the bubbling stops again, brown dust falls down.
This brown dust? Pure gold. Gold sand, gold dust, whatever, it's gold.
Heat it, pour it, and there it is. A real gold bar.
Honest part? One phone gives about three pounds of gold.
So for this bar? About thirty thousand more phones.
Tonne for tonne, old phones still beat a gold mine.
Selling or recycling one? Erase it first. Your photos and bank apps are still inside.
