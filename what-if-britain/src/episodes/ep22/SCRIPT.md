# EP22: Typed Money

Status: script APPROVED 28 Nov 2026 (rev 2) · test render PENDING

Chosen 28 Nov 2026 from the daily brief: the evergreen "basic but a bit complex" pick (where money comes from).
vidIQ had no credits that day, so demand was read from news and search, not from measured outliers.

## The card (read this first)
- **Premise:** Alfie pays his coin jar into the bank. In the what-if Britain, banks can only lend the cash in their
  vault, so his jar goes straight to a stranger's van loan, and the town runs out of cash. The twist: real banks
  don't lend out your money at all. They type brand-new money in.
- **Opener myth, then the real fact:** "you might think it lends your money to someone else" / banks don't simply
  lend out savers' deposits; every new loan creates new money (Bank of England), paid off by the Manager in the twist.
- **Mid-video line:** "And what if Britain's banks could only lend the cash in their vault?" at 0:23 (about 36 %).
- **Twist:** the Manager completes the narrator's sentence ("...that we don't lend out *your* money"), then shows it:
  he types a £20,000 loan into existence, and paying it back makes it vanish.
- **Cliffhanger:** the Manager sees Alfie's pin: "That pin... there's a box in our vault with your *grandad's* na-"
- **Swear (bleeped):** "Oh b*****ks" (the Producer) at about 0:12.
- **New scenes (4):** high-street bank front with a revolving door (bright morning) · the what-if bank hall, full of
  savers' jars · the vault · the rainy high street queue (+ the Booth, about 5 s).
- **Guests:** the Bank Manager (bloated belly on thin legs, pinstripe waistcoat, comb-over, jowls, sweat; voice to
  audition: plummy, smooth, then cracking) · the Builder (slim and wiry, bald, big nose, hi-vis; voice to audition:
  cheery, quick). Both new silhouettes (EP21 used tall-lanky and tiny).
- **Lore:** advances EP21's thread (Nan recognised the pin): the bank has a box in its vault with Grandad's name.
  **Post EP21 first**, because this cliffhanger builds on it.
- **Checks:** 150 words (opener 26) · about 64-66 s · after the redirection 23 lines averaging 5.4 words (max 13) ·
  10 of 23 are questions (43 %) · no line copied from EP20 or EP21.
- **Not advice:** it shows how money is made. It names no bank or product and tells nobody what to do with their
  savings. The description says savings are protected (FSCS) so the bank-run scene doesn't scare anyone.

## Script
Format: `SPEAKER (tone, exaggeration): line`. `SEE:` is what the viewer watches happen. `*word*` turns red in the captions.

### 0:00 BAIT · Scene 1: high-street bank front, bright morning
SEE: Alfie, big in frame, staggers along the high street with a huge frosted coin jar on his shoulder (coins heaped at the neck, clinking). Mate
(heavy-set) jogs ahead to the bank's revolving door, eager to help.
HOOK LINE ON SCREEN (0-3 s): Wait, are your savings lent out?
(The user's change, 28 Nov 2026. It is 6 words, over the 4-word rule, approved on purpose. Grammar: "are" because
"savings" is plural.)
PROP, THE JAR (all jars in the episode): a big sweet-shop jar in tinted, frosted glass, not clear. The coins read as a
heavy mass through the glass, with a heap of shiny coins visible at the open neck and a few stuck to the frosted
side, so the viewer knows at a glance that it's full of money. It needs a soft rim light so the coins catch the light,
and a clink on every move.
NARRATOR (bright, fast, 0.5): If you paid your *savings* into the bank, you might think it lends your money to
someone else, but you might be surprised to know that-

### 0:07 REDIRECTION
SEE: Mate shoves the revolving door far too hard. It spins like a fan, takes Alfie and the jar round three times and
spits them out onto the pavement. The jar smashes and the narrator is cut off mid-word.
SFX: whoosh-whoosh-whoosh, smash. Then 0.8 s of stillness: one £1 coin rolls in a slow circle and topples over.
MATE (sheepish, 0.6): ...Bit *fast*?

### 0:09 REVEAL · The Booth
SEE: The Producer, headset on, clipboard up; the Narrator seen from behind at the mic, frozen mid-gesture.
PRODUCER (off-mic, 0.8): Did the door just *eat* him?
NARRATOR (rattled, 0.7): I hadn't *finished*.
PRODUCER (panicking, 0.9): Oh b*****ks. The pin's *live*!

### 0:14 DROP · Scene 2: the bank hall, switched
SEE: A white crack across the frame; the violet-teal what-if grade. Alfie's pin glows as he crawls through the door,
scooping coins into his taped-up jar. Inside, every saver clutches a labelled jar, and the cashiers' shelves are lined
with more. One jar on the shelf is labelled NAN.
ALFIE (confused, 0.7): Why's everyone holding *jars*?
MATE (squinting, 0.6): Is that... your *nan's* name?

### 0:23 WHAT IF BRITAIN
NARRATOR (recovering, 0.6): And what if Britain's banks could only lend the *cash* in their vault?
SEE: The WHAT IF BRITAIN title card slams in.

### 0:27 ACTIVE LADDER
**Beat 1 · Scene 2: the counter.** SEE: Alfie plonks his jar on the counter. The Bank Manager (bloated belly, thin legs,
comb-over) slaps an ALFIE label on it, then slides it straight along to the next window, where the Builder signs for
a van loan and hugs the jar.
ALFIE (outraged, 0.8): Oi! That's *my* jar!
MANAGER (smug, 0.5): And now it's his *van*.
BUILDER (cheery, 0.7): Cheers, *son*! (He jogs out with it.)

**Beat 2 · Scene 3: the vault.** SEE: Alfie demands to see his money. Mate heaves the giant round vault door open (it
sticks, he strains, it swings and flattens him against the wall). The vault is enormous and almost empty: a handful of
jars on one shelf. A board on the wall: CASH: £3 IN EVERY £100. FACT: shown on the board for 2 s and said.
ALFIE (alarmed, 0.7): Where's everyone *else's*?
MANAGER (whisper, 0.5): Only *three* quid in a hundred is cash.
MATE (peeling off the wall, 0.6): Then where's the other *ninety-seven*?

**Beat 3 · Scene 4: the high street, rain.** SEE: Word spreads. A queue of umbrellas snakes down the rainy high street.
Nan (tiny, stooped) is at the front, banging on the door with her walking stick. The Manager slams the shutter down.
NAN (furious, 0.8): I want my *savings*, now!
MANAGER (panicking, 0.9): Everyone wants them *now*!
ALFIE (rising panic, 0.8): So nobody's got their *money*?

### 0:50 TWIST
SEE: Behind the shutter, the Manager drags Alfie to an old computer. For a split second the grade flickers to normal
daylight, the real Britain. He taps the keyboard: on screen the Builder's balance jumps from £0 to £20,000, and no jar
moves anywhere. Then the dropout: the bed cuts out.
MANAGER (low, 0.6): ...that we don't lend out *your* money.
ALFIE (0.8): Then where's his loan *from*?
MANAGER (0.5): I *typed* it.
MATE (disbelief, 0.8): You can just *do* that?
MANAGER (0.6): And when he pays it back, it *vanishes*.
SEE: he taps once more; the £20,000 drops back to £0.

### 0:59 CLIFFHANGER
SEE: Alfie's pin flickers violently. The Manager freezes, staring at it, the sweat running.
PRODUCER (off-mic, shouting, 1.1): Pull him *out*! Now!
MANAGER (shocked, 1.2): That pin... there's a box in our vault with your *grandad's* na-
CUT TO BLACK
