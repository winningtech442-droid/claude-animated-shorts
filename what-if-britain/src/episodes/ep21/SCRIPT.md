# EP21: Mattress Money (v3 pilot)

Status: script APPROVED 27 Nov 2026 · test render PENDING

## The card (read this first)
- **Premise:** Alfie hides £1,000 under his mattress. In the what-if Britain, money visibly shrinks as prices rise.
  The twist: it shrinks in the real Britain too; you just can't see it.
- **Opener myth, then the real fact:** "you might think you'd still have a grand" / cash kept since 2016 has lost
  more than a quarter of what it buys (paid off by Nan in the twist).
- **Mid-video line:** "What if Britain's money shrank as fast as its prices rise?" at 0:23 (36 %).
- **Twist:** the scary shrinking-money world is ours, made visible. Nan completes the narrator's cut-off sentence.
- **Cliffhanger:** Nan sees Alfie's pin: "That pin... your grandad had one just like i-"
- **Swear (bleeped):** "Oh s***" (the Producer) at 0:12.
- **New scenes (4):** Alfie's bedroom · corner shop · high-street electronics shop (Black Friday sale) · Nan's kitchen
  (+ the Booth, about 5 s).
- **Guests:** the Shopkeeper (tall and lanky, cardigan, deadpan) · the Sales Assistant (tiny, bored, lanyard).
  Both need a voice approved before voicing.
- **Lore:** new thread: Nan recognises the pin. Did Grandad have one?
- **Checks:** 138 words (opener 28) · about 64 s · after the redirection 22 lines averaging 5.0 words (max 11) · 8 of 22 are
  questions (36 %).
- **Not advice:** it shows how money shrinks and what keeps it its size; it never names a product or tells anyone
  what to buy.

## Script
Format: `SPEAKER (tone, exaggeration): line`. `SEE:` is what the viewer watches happen. `*word*` turns red in the captions.

### 0:00 BAIT · Scene 1: Alfie's bedroom, terraced house, bright morning
SEE: Alfie, big in frame, stuffs a fat envelope of £20 notes under his mattress. Mate (heavy-set) watches from the
doorway, eating toast.
HOOK LINE ON SCREEN (0-3 s): £1,000 under the mattress?
NARRATOR (bright, fast, 0.5): If you hid a *thousand* pounds under your mattress for ten years, you might think you'd
still have a grand, but you might be surprised to know that-

### 0:07 REDIRECTION
SEE: Mate flops onto the bed to "help". The springs fire Alfie across the room into the wardrobe. The narrator is cut
off mid-word.
SFX: boing, crash. Then 0.8 s of stillness: one £20 note see-saws down to the carpet.
MATE (muffled, 0.6): ...Sorry.

### 0:09 REVEAL · The Booth
SEE: The Producer, headset on, clipboard flapping; the Narrator seen from behind at the mic, tea spilling.
PRODUCER (off-mic, 0.8): Is he *okay*?
NARRATOR (rattled, 0.7): That wasn't in the *script*.
PRODUCER (panicking, 0.9): Oh s***. His *pin*.

### 0:14 DROP · Scene 1 again, switched
SEE: A white crack across the frame; the violet-teal what-if grade. Alfie's pin glows as he crawls out of the
wardrobe. His £20 notes have shrunk to the size of stamps.
ALFIE (confused, 0.7): Why's my money *tiny*?
MATE (squinting, 0.6): Is that... a *twenty*?

### 0:23 WHAT IF BRITAIN
NARRATOR (recovering, 0.6): What if Britain's money *shrank* as fast as its prices rise?
SEE: The WHAT IF BRITAIN title card slams in.

### 0:27 ACTIVE LADDER
**Beat 1 · Scene 2: corner shop.** SEE: Alfie slaps a tiny tenner on the counter for milk and bread. The Shopkeeper
(tall, lanky) slides half the shopping back. The till receipt reads "2016: £10 / NOW: about £14".
SHOPKEEPER (deadpan, 0.5): That's *2016* money, son.
ALFIE (outraged, 0.8): It's still *ten* quid!
SHOPKEEPER (0.5): Not in *here* it isn't.

**Beat 2 · Scene 3: high-street electronics shop, Black Friday banners.** SEE: Mate charges in and wedges himself in
the doorway with a giant TV box marked -70 %. The price tag flips: same price in July. Card: "94 % of deals: same
price or cheaper other times (Which?)".
MATE (thrilled, 0.9): Seventy percent *off*!
SALES ASSISTANT (bored, 0.4): Same price as *July*.
MATE (deflating, 0.7): Then off *what*?

**Beat 3 · Scene 4: Nan's kitchen.** SEE: Nan (tiny, stooped, huge glasses) prises open two tins. In the old biscuit
tin her notes have shrunk to crumbs. In the jar labelled SAVINGS they are still full size.
ALFIE (amazed, 0.7): Nan, why's yours still *big*?
NAN (warm, 0.5): It's been earning *faster* than the prices, love.

### 0:50 TWIST
SEE: Nan holds Alfie's tiny tenner up to the window with a magnifying glass. For a split second the grade flickers to
normal daylight and the note looks full size again.
NAN (gentle, 0.6): ...that it shrinks in the *real* Britain too.
ALFIE (0.8): By how *much*?
NAN (0.6): More than a *quarter*, in ten years.
ALFIE (rising panic, 0.9): Then why can't I *see* it?
NAN (0.5): Nobody prints it *smaller*, love.

### 0:58 CLIFFHANGER
SEE: Alfie's pin flickers violently. Nan freezes, staring at it.
PRODUCER (off-mic, shouting, 1.1): Get him *back*! Now!
NAN (shocked, 1.2): That pin... your *grandad* had one just like i-
CUT TO BLACK
