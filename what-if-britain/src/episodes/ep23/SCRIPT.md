# EP23: Pay Later (global edition)

Status: script APPROVED 28 Nov 2026 (rev 1) · guest voices APPROVED 28 Nov · shots built on placeholder timings · voices PENDING · test render PENDING

Chosen 28 Nov 2026 from the daily brief. The user asked for a global target: something in demand, or a basic-but-a-bit-
complex money idea. The pick is pay-later ("pay in 4"), timed for Cyber Monday (Mon 30 Nov). vidIQ had no credits,
so demand was judged from news and search (Adobe forecasts record pay-later spending this season).
**Global edition (the user's choice):** Alfie, the pin, the Booth and "What if Britain...?" stay. Prices are in
dollars, the places read anywhere, every fact holds worldwide, and no provider is named.

## The card (read this first)
- **Premise:** Alfie splits a $100 jacket into four payments. In the what-if Britain the tills only take pay-later, so
  everything is "free today" until "later" arrives through the letterbox. The twist: the shop pays the pay-later
  firm up to $8 in every $100, because you buy more.
- **Opener myth, then the real fact:** "you might think nobody's making money, because there's no interest" / the shop
  pays the pay-later firm a fee of about 2-8 % of the sale (cards cost it 1.5-3.5 %). The Owner pays it off in the twist.
- **Mid-video line:** "And what if Britain's tills only took *pay later*?" at 0:23 (about 36 %).
- **Twist:** the Shop Owner completes the narrator's sentence ("...that the *shop* pays them"), shows the receipt
  (JACKET $100 / FEE -$8 / SHOP $92) and says why: "Because you buy *more*."
- **Cliffhanger:** the pay-later Rep scans Alfie's pin: "This pin's still on the never-never... your *grandad* owes
  three payme-"
- **Swear (bleeped):** "B****y hell" (the Producer) at about 0:13.
- **New scenes (4):** department-store sale floor (bright morning; same set re-dressed for the what-if) · Mate's front
  hall · car boot sale in the rain · the store's back office (+ the Booth, about 5 s). The sale floor is clothes rails
  and escalators, not EP21's electronics shop.
- **Guests (both new silhouettes; EP21-22 used tall-lanky, tiny, bloated belly on thin legs, slim and wiry):**
  - **The Pay-Later Rep:** short and square, barrel chest, no neck, slicked-back hair, a huge white grin; teal gilet
    over a white shirt, card reader on a lanyard; scoots into every shot on a kick scooter. Voice to audition:
    smooth, upbeat salesman, quick.
  - **The Shop Owner:** pear-shaped (narrow sloping shoulders, wide hips), bald with a horseshoe of grey hair, walrus
    moustache, brown shop coat, glasses on a chain, sweaty. Voice to audition: weary, gravelly, deadpan.
- **Lore (approved with the script, 28 Nov):** advances the Grandad thread from EP21 (Nan knew the pin) and EP22 (a box in the vault with
  Grandad's name): Grandad got the pin on the never-never and three payments are still owed. It opens a new question:
  who sold it to him? **Post EP21 and EP22 first.**
- **Checks:** 146 words (opener 27, about 4 words a second) · about 62-65 s · after the redirection 24 lines averaging
  5.0 words (max 11) · 11 of 24 are questions (46 %) · no line copied from EP21 or EP22, none repeated.
- **Not advice:** it shows who pays for "0 %". It names no provider and tells nobody whether to use pay-later. The
  description says pay-in-4 is interest-free when paid on time, that missed payments can cost a fee, and that rules
  differ by country.

## Script
Format: `SPEAKER (tone, exaggeration): line`. `SEE:` is what the viewer watches happen. `*word*` turns red in the captions.

### 0:00 BAIT · Scene 1: department-store sale floor, bright morning
SEE: A big, bright sale floor: rails of coats, an escalator, SALE banners (no brand, no currency on the banners).
Alfie, big in frame, is already sprinting with the last red puffer jacket hugged to his chest, a big $100 tag
swinging off it. Impact on frame 1: he skids into the till (trainer squeak, thud). Mate (heavy-set) barrels in behind
with an overloaded trolley. The card reader on the counter glows: PAY IN 4.
HOOK LINE ON SCREEN (0-3 s): Is pay later free?
NARRATOR (bright, fast, 0.5): If you split a hundred-dollar jacket into four payments, you might think nobody's making
money, because there's no interest, but you might be surprised to know that-

### 0:07 REDIRECTION
SEE: Alfie taps PAY IN 4. Mate, keen to help, grabs the other end of the jacket to bag it. Tug of war. The jacket rips
into four neat pieces with a huge RRRIP, and the narrator is cut off mid-word. A sleeve flops onto Alfie's head.
SFX: the rip, then 0.8 s of stillness while the bed keeps playing: the $100 tag, torn in four, flutters down and lands.
MATE (sheepish, 0.6): ...You said *split* it.

### 0:09 REVEAL · The Booth
SEE: The Producer, headset on, clipboard up; the Narrator seen from behind at the mic, frozen mid-gesture.
PRODUCER (off-mic, 0.8): Did he just *rip* the jacket?
NARRATOR (rattled, 0.7): Split the *payments*! Not the jacket!
PRODUCER (panicking, 0.9): B****y hell. He's *switching*!

### 0:14 DROP · Scene 1, switched
SEE: A white crack across the frame; the violet-teal what-if grade; Alfie's pin glows. The jacket is whole again on
the rail. Every till now has one giant button, PAY LATER: no cash drawer, no card slot. Shoppers sprint past with TVs,
a sofa, a rowing machine, and nobody pays. Alfie still has the sleeve on his head.
ALFIE (confused, 0.7): Where's the *cash* drawer?
MATE (gawping, 0.6): Why's everyone carrying a *sofa*?

### 0:23 WHAT IF BRITAIN
NARRATOR (recovering, 0.6): And what if Britain's tills only took *pay later*?
SEE: The WHAT IF BRITAIN title card slams in.

### 0:27 ACTIVE LADDER
**Beat 1 · Scene 1, switched: the till.** SEE: Mate shoves up a trolley the size of a car: a telly, a fridge, a garden
gnome, a jet ski and a tower of boxes (twelve things in all). He slaps PAY LATER and the till pings "$0 TODAY" for every item. The trolley creeps forward and rolls
over Alfie's foot.
MATE (delighted, 0.8): It's all *free*!
ALFIE (wincing, 0.7): It's not free. It's *later*!

**Beat 2 · Scene 2: Mate's front hall, "two weeks later".** SEE: The letterbox fires a stream of envelopes, each one
marked PAYMENT 2 OF 4. They pile up to the ceiling; Alfie and Mate dig through with snow shovels. The doorbell: the
Pay-Later Rep scoots in on his kick scooter, grinning, card reader on his lanyard. He slaps a red LATE FEE sticker on
Mate's forehead. FACT: the sticker holds for 2 s.
ALFIE (alarmed, 0.7): How many payments *is* that?
MATE (counting on his fingers, 0.6): Twelve things, times four... *forty-eight*?
REP (cheery, 0.7): And you missed *one*.
MATE (outraged, 0.8): A *late* fee?

**Beat 3 · Scene 3: car boot sale, rain.** SEE: Alfie and Mate shiver at a car boot sale, trying to flog the jet ski
and the gnome to cover the payments. Nan (tiny, stooped) pokes the jet ski with her walking stick and raises an eyebrow.
NAN (knowing, 0.6): In my day, we called this the *never-never*.
ALFIE (0.7): The never-*what*?
NAN (dry, 0.6): You *never* finish paying.
ALFIE (rising panic, 0.8): Then who's getting *rich*?
SEE: Mate points across the car park at the department store's lit-up windows.

### 0:49 TWIST · Scene 4: the store's back office
SEE: A cramped back office: boxes, an old adding machine. For a split second the grade flickers to normal daylight,
the real world. The Shop Owner (pear-shaped, walrus moustache) slumps over a long receipt and holds it up to the
camera: JACKET $100 / FEE -$8 / SHOP $92. Then the dropout: the bed cuts out.
OWNER (weary, 0.6): ...that the *shop* pays them.
ALFIE (stunned, 0.8): You pay *eight* dollars?
OWNER (0.6): Up to eight, *every* hundred.
MATE (0.7): So why *offer* it?
OWNER (deadpan, 0.6): Because you buy *more*.
SEE: hard cut to Mate's trolley, the jet ski on top; Mate quietly hides the gnome behind his back.

### 0:58 CLIFFHANGER
SEE: The Rep bursts in on his scooter and swipes his card reader past Alfie's pin. BEEP. The reader flashes red. The
pin flickers violently.
PRODUCER (off-mic, shouting, 1.1): Don't let him *scan* it!
REP (shocked, 1.2): This pin's still on the never-never... your *grandad* owes three payme-
CUT TO BLACK
