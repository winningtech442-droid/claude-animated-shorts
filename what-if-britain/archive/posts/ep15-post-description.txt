What if your pension shortcut stopped at two thousand pounds? Salary sacrifice lets your employer pay part of your salary into your pension before National Insurance is taken. Under the National Insurance Contributions (Employer Pensions Contributions) Act 2026, from 6 April 2029 only the first £2,000 a year stays free of National Insurance, while income tax relief stays. Our own sum at today's rates: a £50,000 salary with £5,000 sacrificed has £3,000 over the cap, so about £240 a year less take-home pay, and roughly £450 more for the employer.
