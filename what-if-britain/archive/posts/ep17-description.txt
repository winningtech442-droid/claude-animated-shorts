YOUR £100 ENERGY PAYMENT… GONE BY MARCH?

Twenty million homes in council tax bands A to D in England are getting a £100 Household Energy Payment from January 2027, paid straight into your bank if you pay council tax by direct debit. But January is also when the energy price cap moves, and forecasts point to the biggest jump in years. Back in 2022 the same homes got a £150 council tax rebate plus £400 off every energy bill. This winter it's just the hundred. We played it out from New Year's Day to March, watched the smart meter sprint and the radiator eat the cash, then ran our own sum on a typical bill. Should winter energy help follow your pay instead of your house's council tax band? Follow for the real number when Ofgem confirms the January price cap, and share this with whoever pays the bills in your house. Hypothetical scenario, not financial advice.
