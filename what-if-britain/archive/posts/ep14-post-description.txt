What if your pension pot walked into your estate? In Britain, unused pension pots have always sat outside the estate for inheritance tax. Under the Finance Act 2026, from 6 April 2027 most unused pensions count towards inheritance tax on death, with 40% charged above the £325,000 nil-rate band, or £500,000 when a home passes to children.
