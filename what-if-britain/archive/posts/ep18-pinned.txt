Fair play to anyone who spots it: "she's won every single year" isn't quite right. In April 2022 the earnings link was suspended for one year, so the pension rose 3.1% while pay grew about 8.3%. Every other year since 2011 it matched or beat pay (House of Commons Library). The 89% is the basic State Pension: £97.65 a week in 2010-11, £184.90 now (GOV.UK). The £5,500 is our own sum: typical full-time pay (£40,911, ONS 2026) grown 89% instead of 66% since 2010. Your own pay history will differ.
