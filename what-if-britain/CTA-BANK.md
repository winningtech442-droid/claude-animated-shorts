## How a good ask sounds here

- It sounds like Alfie talking to a mate, not like a presenter reading out a list.
- It comes from that episode: it reuses the episode's number, its argument or its next step.
- It gives a reason ("so tomorrow's finds you", "for the next pub argument"), not just an order.
- It's short: 8-14 words, said in one breath.
- It's shown, not only said: Alfie acts it out (the actions are listed below), in the same caption style as the rest of the video.
- The post description ends on the same ask, worded a little differently.
- No bait: never "like if you agree", "comment YES", "follow for follow", "share or bad luck" or "share if you're British".
- **A follow must promise something real.** Only tease a next topic that is already scripted, and only say "tomorrow" if it posts tomorrow.

## The bank

### Share (lead ask, even episodes: aim it at one specific person the viewer knows)
A share works when the viewer instantly thinks of someone. Name the person, not the button.
| # | Line | Alfie on screen |
|---|---|---|
| SH1 | Know someone who {belief from the episode}? Send them this. | Holds up his phone, eyebrows raised, thumb hovering |
| SH2 | Send this to whoever pays the mortgage in your house. | Slides a letter across the kitchen table to Mate |
| SH3 | Send this to the friend who's just remortgaged. Or don't. Their face, though. | Winces, then grins |
| SH4 | Got a Nan who remembers this? Send it to her, and ask her. | Nan's phone buzzes; she peers at it over her glasses |
| SH5 | Send this to the family group chat and watch it kick off. | Phone pinging nonstop on the table |
| SH6 | Send this to the one mate who still thinks {myth}. | Points at Mate; Mate shrugs, caught out |
| SH7 | Share this with anyone who's ever moaned about {bill}. | Waves the bill like a flag |
| SH8 | Send this to someone who's buying their first home. They'll want to see that number. | Hands over a house key on a ribbon |

### Follow (lead ask, odd episodes: give a reason to come back)
A follow works when the viewer wants the next thing. Name the next thing, not the button.
| # | Line | Alfie on screen |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | There's a new what-if here every day. Follow, and tomorrow's finds you. | Walks off down the street, turns back and points at camera |
| F2 | Next: what if {next topic}? Follow, so you don't miss it. | Holds up tomorrow's newspaper, headline half-hidden |
| F3 | Stick around. Britain nearly went a hundred different ways. | Opens a door onto a long corridor of doors |
| F4 | Follow, and I'll show you what {tease} cost you tomorrow. | Mate leans in to whisper; Alfie holds up a finger: "tomorrow" |
| F5 | Part two's tomorrow: {part-two hook}. Follow so you catch it. | Tears a calendar page; tomorrow's date is circled in red |
| F6 | I've got {next topic} lined up next. Follow, and it'll be waiting. | Pats a sealed envelope marked "NEXT", slides it into his jacket |
| F7 | Every one of these changed your money. Follow for the rest. | Flicks through a stack of old receipts like a flipbook |
| F8 | Nan's got more where that came from. Follow, she's only getting started. | Nan winks and taps a shoebox of old bills |

### Save (rotating, for episodes with a number worth keeping or an argument worth winning)
| # | Line | Alfie on screen |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Save this for the next pub argument about {topic}. | Slaps the bar with a smug grin, then taps his jacket pocket |
| S2 | Save this one. It'll win you Christmas dinner. | Pulls a cracker; a paper hat lands on his head (Dec only) |
| S3 | Save this for the next time someone says {myth}. | Folds the newspaper and tucks it under his arm |
| S4 | Pop this in your saves. You'll want that {number} later. | Pins the price tag to a cork board |

### Comment (rotating, for episodes that split people into sides)
| # | Line | Alfie on screen |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Nan remembers this one. Do you? Tell me what it cost you. | Nan nods knowingly over her glasses |
| C2 | {Side A} or {side B}? Pick a side in the comments. | Alfie and Mate face off, arms folded |
| C3 | Chancellor for a day: what would you have done? I'm reading them all. | Alfie sits at an empty podium, pen ready |
| C4 | Would you have done it differently? Make your case below. | Points down at the comments, eyebrows up |

### Like (rotating, for episodes with a big shock number or a feel-good twist)
| # | Line | Alfie on screen |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | If that number made you wince, give it a like and I'll dig up more. | Winces at the number card, then gives a thumbs up |
| L2 | Learnt something about your own money? A like tells me to keep going. | Nods, taps his temple |
| L3 | Like this if you'd want that {number} back. | Holds out an empty wallet |
| L4 | A like keeps these coming. There's plenty more Britain that nearly was. | Flicks a coin, catches it |

### Two-ask lines (use once every 4-5 videos at most)
| # | Line |
|---|---|
| D1 | Save this, and follow for part two. |
| D2 | Tell me your side, and follow to see who was right tomorrow. |
| D3 | Tell me your side, then send it to someone who'll disagree. |
| D4 | Save this, and send it to whoever needs to hear it. |
| D5 | Send this to {person}, and follow for tomorrow's. |

Fill the {slots} from the episode. A line can be reused, but no more than once every 10 videos.
