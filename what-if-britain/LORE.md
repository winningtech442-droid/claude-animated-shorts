# What If Britain: lore (continuity bible)

Read before writing any script. Every episode stands alone, so a new viewer never needs this; the lore is the
reward for regulars. Never contradict the canon below. Add a line to the log after every posted episode.

## Canon (decided 27 Nov 2026)
- **Alfie's Union Jack pin is the timeline switch.** When it fires (white crack, violet-teal grade, the pin glows),
  Alfie drops into a what-if Britain. Not yet revealed: where the pin came from, why it fires, how he gets back.
- **The Booth.** The show is presented from a booth by the Narrator (heard, never seen from the front) with a Producer.
  They can see and hear Alfie, and things keep going off-script. Their names get fixed in the pilot.
- **Mate** is the sceptic ("but how?"). **Nan** remembers the old prices.

## Rules
- Each cliffhanger adds one thread or advances one. Pay off or advance an old thread at least every 4 episodes.
- A thread is only canon once it has been posted. Ideas below are not canon until used.
