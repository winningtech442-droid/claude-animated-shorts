Your phone's brain is made of sand.
Sand. Inside your phone. Thinking for you.
Giant factories do this. Never at home.
Only three steps. I said that last time.
First, cook it with coal. Out comes grey metal.
Still way, way too dirty for a chip.
So they clean it, clean it, and clean it again.
A gold bar? Four nines pure. This? Nine nines, even eleven.
Now melt it. Hotter than lava.
Dip in a tiny seed. Pull it up, slowly, slowly.
Spin it one way, the pot the other. Or the other way. Whatever.
Slower than a snail. About two days.
Out comes one giant glowing crystal, tall as you.
Heavy as a piano. Perfect, all the way through.
A wire saw slices it like cheese, thin as a bank card.
Polish it to a mirror. Hundreds of phone brains fit on one.
Honest part? Not beach sand. Pure quartz rock. Most sand's too dirty.
Rock or sand, mostly the same stuff. Still thinking for you.
Phone hot while charging? Take the case off.
Did you know your phone started as rock? Yes or no below.
